- 在现 HEAD `engineering/f06/transaction-priority-admission-ci/service.mjs` 的 `admissibility`，遍历 `public.transactions` 与 `f06_3b_ci.transaction_origins` 时写有：`if(signed.status==='in_progress'&&t.status!=='in_progress') return F06_5C_PUBLIC_PARENT_TRANSACTION_STATE_DRIFT`。
